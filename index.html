THE TALE OF THE TWINS

HOW TO PLAY

✦ ONE INPUT, TWO SPIRITS: Every directional command guides both Ember (Lilac Witch) and Tide (Teal Mage). Their steps are linked by mirror rules!

✦ WALL BLOCKS: If one spirit hits a bush wall, it stays still while its twin still moves. Use walls to desynchronize your paths!

✦ CONTROLS: WASD or Arrow Keys for straight moves. Diagonals: QEZC, Numpad, or pressing two arrows within 80ms. Or drag the on-screen joystick!

✦ HAZARDS: Entering flame, water, electric or arrow tiles costs 1 heart. If either spirit reaches 0 hearts, the level restarts.

✦ GOAL: Guide Ember and Tide to their portals simultaneously to triumph!
